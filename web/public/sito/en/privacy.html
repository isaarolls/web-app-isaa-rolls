Privacy notice

Data controller: Isaa Rolls.

Event requests

When you fill in the “Request a quote” form we process your name, phone number, e-mail and the event details you give us, only to reply to you and prepare the quote (legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and, if the event is confirmed, to meet our legal obligations. We do not share them with third parties and do not use them for advertising.

Bike location

The map only shows the location of our bike, while we are open. The website does not ask for or record your location. The map is provided by OpenStreetMap and the Leaflet library is loaded from unpkg.com: to display it, your browser connects to these services, which receive your IP address.

Cookies

This website does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data by writing to us, and you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
